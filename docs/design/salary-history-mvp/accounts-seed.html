Welcome to Salary History

Salary History tracks your payslips over time. Before you can record a payslip, you need at least one account (e.g. "Primary Salary", "Side Job", etc.). You can always rename or delete it later.

First-run only: this prompt appears once when the accounts table is empty. Skip it and you can create accounts later from the Accounts section.

Skip lets you start the app without any accounts; the next payslip entry will prompt for account selection. Cancel dismisses without saving (you'll see this prompt again on next activation). Create Account inserts into the accounts table (Phase 4 Shared Financial Data), writes is_active=true, and shows the payslip form.
